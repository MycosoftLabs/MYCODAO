## Projects

Each MycoDAO project — Project Oyster, Project Lion, Project Fusarium, LAB-IN-A-BOX and others — has a page with its summary, milestones, funding needs and updates.

## Funding needs

A funding need states:

- **Purpose** — what the money buys (for example, sequencing one batch of cultures).
- **Amount and asset** — once approved; a target is labeled as a target.
- **Recipient** — who receives the funds.
- **Stage** — target → approved budget → reserved → committed → paid.
- **Next step** — how to support it today, if that is possible.

## Following a project

Open a project from the Projects tab. Milestones show whether evidence is attached. Funding needs show how far money has actually moved. Updates link to the records behind each change.

## Example

Project Oyster studies salt tolerance and bioremediation potential in pink oyster mushrooms (*Pleurotus djamor*). Its proposed MYCO pilot is one sequencing batch. It stays a target until the budget, recipient and terms are approved.

## Three ways value comes back

1. **Rewards at discovery (beta).** Species tokens or MYCO for accepted observations.
2. **Bounties for specimens (planned).** Projects pay for specific, accepted specimens or cultures.
3. **Benefit sharing from agreements (planned).** When a licence or supply agreement produces income, the agreement can name a share for the discoverer, the biobank, and the country of origin where the Nagoya Protocol applies.

## Why agreements, not tokens

Benefit sharing has to be written into contracts that name the parties, the asset and the payment terms. A token can record that a contribution happened. It cannot by itself create a legal right to income.

## What holders should expect

MYCO governance can direct treasury funds to rewards and bounties. Holding MYCO does not entitle anyone to a share of licensing income. That would need approved terms, which have not been published.

## The full loop

**Find** fungi → **record** and **culture** them → **study** their compounds and materials → **make** products with partners → **pay** discoverers and fund the next search. Every turn of the loop adds to the world's fungal catalog.

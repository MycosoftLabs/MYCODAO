## Identity

- **Network:** Solana (SPL token)
- **Mint:** `EzYEwn4R5tNkNGw4K2a5a58MJFQESdf1r4UJrV7cpUF3`
- **Total supply:** 210,000,000 MYCO
- **Governance:** the MycoDAO Realm on Solana

Always check the mint address before you trade. Tokens with the same name and a different mint are not MYCO.

## What MYCO does today

| Use | Stage |
|---|---|
| Vote on MycoDAO proposals in Realms | Beta |
| Earn rewards for accepted species observations | Beta (Solana devnet) |
| Pay for biobank storage or tissue samples | Planned |
| Support a documented project need | Planned |
| Commercial or licensing rights | None |

## What MYCO is not

Holding MYCO does **not** give royalty, revenue-sharing, dividend or ownership rights in any species, specimen, compound or company. The MYCO specification is awaiting approval; until it is published, treat any claim of economic rights as unapproved.

## Four planned flows

1. **Discovery rewards (beta).** A forager files an observation against a MINDEX taxon. After review, the species reward policy decides the payout: that species' token for a known species, or MYCO for eligible new or rare records.
2. **Service payment (planned).** Labs and companies pay in MYCO to store cultures, request tissue samples, or commission sequencing from the biobank.
3. **Project support (planned).** Holders contribute MYCO to a named funding need, such as one sequencing batch for Project Oyster, and follow the result on the project page.
4. **Treasury inflows (planned).** Grants, service fees and licensing income under named agreements flow to the treasury, which funds the next round of rewards and bounties.

## Published allocation

The published allocation of the 210,000,000 supply is: community and research grants 30%, biobank and data incentives 22%, industry partnerships 18%, liquidity and operations 12%, and founding team 18%. These are allocations, not spending records; actual transfers are on-chain.

## Why this closes the loop

Rewards and bounties pay discoverers, services and partnerships bring value back in, and governance decides how the treasury is spent. Each flow only becomes *available* once a real recipient, budget, fulfilment process and support owner exist. Until then it stays labeled *planned*.

## Where MYCO trades

MYCO's main market is a **MYCO/SOL concentrated-liquidity pool on Raydium**, created on May 12, 2025. Aggregators such as Jupiter route swaps through it. Daily price history for the pool is public on GeckoTerminal and shown on the Pulse and MYCO tabs.

## Liquidity

A pool's *liquidity* is how much MYCO and SOL sit in it. With little liquidity, small trades move the price a lot (high *price impact*), and some trackers, DexScreener for example, stop listing the pair. The token and its history still exist; the market is just thin until liquidity is added.

## Before you trade

- Confirm the mint address.
- Check price impact and set a slippage limit.
- Remember that swaps are irreversible.
- Treat MYCO as a participation token, not an investment with promised returns.
